# Content notes

Reviewed on September 16, 2026. The generator contains 660 original, player-directed story prompts, including 278 new pack-specific ideas. Pack coverage is 21 Expansion Packs, 12 Game Packs and 20 Stuff Packs. Kits, Creator Kits, Marketplace Maker Packs and the Digital Deluxe upgrade are outside this catalogue.

Every entry carries its required pack IDs and an explicit game reference. Cross-pack prompts show each requirement. Base Game means the current, updated base game. Character availability depends on the player's save. Stories about rivalries, careers, households and secrets are challenges to enact, not promises of automatic game events. Premade venues such as Chez Llama and Oasis Springs Dust Bowl must be placed before visiting.
